Detection models shipped in this folder

  checker.pt, checker.onnx   Aero-GCP checkerboard target detector ("Aero V1.0")
  spray.pt,   spray.onnx     Aero-GCP spray-mark target detector ("Aero V1.0")

What they are
  YOLO11n models, fine-tuned by HELIX AERO from the yolo11n.pt weights of Ultralytics, with the Ultralytics training code.
  The .onnx files are the same two models converted to ONNX.
  The images used for the fine-tuning are not part of this repository.
